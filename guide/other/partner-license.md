# Partner License

Pill Engine is free to use under the Pill Engine Community License. Once your
annual revenue reaches USD 300,000, that license asks you to publish the
changes you make to the engine itself.

The Partner License is the paid alternative. You sign one annual agreement
with the licensor and keep your engine changes private at any revenue. Your
game or product is unaffected: you still own it, still set its price and
still release it under your own terms.

It is always a choice, never a requirement. Above the threshold you can also
stay on the Community License and publish your engine changes, or use Pill
Engine under the EUPL-1.2.

Partner fees fund the development of Pill Engine, which stays free for
everyone.

## Indicative pricing

The fee is an annual amount set by your revenue band. It is not a share of
your product revenue. If your revenue moves to another band, the new band
applies from your next renewal.

| Annual Revenue                       | Indicative annual fee |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| USD 300,000 to less than 1,000,000   | USD 12,000            |
| USD 1,000,000 to less than 2,000,000 | USD 36,000            |
| USD 2,000,000 to less than 4,000,000 | USD 80,000            |
| USD 4,000,000 to USD 6,000,000       | USD 140,000           |
| More than USD 6,000,000              | Set individually      |

Fees are invoiced annually in advance; the signed agreement states any
applicable taxes or proration.

The first 10 Partners to sign and pay get a 50% discount on the annual fee
for their first 4 years. Their undiscounted revenue bands are locked at the
rates in effect when they sign.

> These amounts are an invitation to discuss terms, not an offer. The fee in
> the signed agreement is the one that applies, and this pricing may change.

## Getting a Partner License

A Partner License exists only as a written agreement signed by you and the
licensor. Write to [contact@pillengine.org](mailto:contact@pillengine.org) to
start the discussion.

The full summary of terms, including the rights granted and the limitations,
is the `legal/licenses/PARTNER_LICENSE.md` file in the Pill Engine
repository.
